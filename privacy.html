Privacy Policy

This policy outlines how ZenLyft handles, processes, and protects your information when you visit our website or interact with our digital products.

1. Information Collection

ZenLyft collects information provided directly by users, such as contact details submitted via inquiry forms, account registration information across our products, and communication preferences.

2. Website Usage & Analytics

When you access our website, we automatically collect basic browser diagnostics, device information, and anonymous usage telemetry to ensure reliable performance and improve navigation.

3. Product Information Processing

For users interacting with specific ZenLyft products such as ZenLyft AutoMatch, data processing is governed by user-submitted parameters (e.g. skill profiles, work history, or organization job descriptions) strictly for delivering relevant algorithmic matching.

4. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

We use essential cookies to maintain secure sessions and preserve user preferences. We do not sell personal data or track users across third-party websites for advertising.

5. Data Security

We implement industry-standard administrative, physical, and technical safeguards designed to protect personal and corporate data against unauthorized access, loss, or disclosure.

6. Third-Party Services

ZenLyft may utilize trusted cloud hosting infrastructure and essential API providers. These third parties are bound by strict confidentiality and data handling agreements.

7. User Rights

You have the right to request access to your personal data, request correction of inaccurate records, or request deletion of personal information where applicable by law.

8. Data Retention

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected, resolve disputes, and comply with legal requirements.

9. Contact Information

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy or wish to exercise your data rights, please contact the ZenLyft team via our official contact page.
